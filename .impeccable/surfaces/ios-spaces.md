# Native iOS spaces

- Mode: Operate. Native SwiftUI on iPhone; Windows owns code, Mac owns Xcode and simulator verification.
- Authority: User-approved Today and Calendar establish graphite semantic surfaces, precise blue actions, system typography and restrained numeric summaries. Their content/layout remains unchanged; only the requested profile entry joins their navigation.
- Scope: Nutrition, More hub, Notes, Sport, Goals, Work, Travel, Health and Affairs. Reuse existing data, editors, persistence and offline behavior. No invented live integrations or sample records outside the local test account.
- Direction: A compact native inline navigation title, profile on the left and a contextual plus on the right. Profile opens account, settings, backups and help. More starts directly with space tiles; hold/drag customizes their order per account.
- Nutrition: Date navigation; compact eaten/remaining/target calorie summary; protein/carbohydrate/fat amounts and remaining targets; a small water control; breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks, each with totals and direct creation. Existing analysis, targets, catalog and saved meals remain reachable.
- Modules: Notes prioritize search, folders and pinned notes. Sport prioritizes week and planned/completed sessions. Goals prioritize meaningful progress and categories. Work prioritizes focus and project tasks. Travel prioritizes trip readiness and itinerary/packing. Health prioritizes daily check-in and reminders. Affairs keeps distinct matters, finances, documents and vehicles.
- Interaction contract: Tap opens or edits the record; hold exposes named actions. A short horizontal swipe acts on that record, without hijacking vertical scrolling. Drag changes a real relationship (meal category, note folder, workout date, goal category, work project/status, itinerary order), with equivalent menu actions where practical. Destruction is confirmed or undoable. Never invent a drag for a screen where movement has no meaning.
- Quality bar: Main purpose and add action visible immediately; compact summaries instead of oversized decorative metrics; no duplicate screen titles; native navigation and keyboard behavior; Dynamic Type, VoiceOver action names and minimum 44pt touch targets. Verify real preview data on the persistent iPhone simulator, one batched visual review and one correction pass.
